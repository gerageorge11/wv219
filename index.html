A note from the north

Welcome to our home far from home.

For one edition, Veliky Novgorod becomes a gateway to Tamriel. Between the old city walls and the snow-covered mountains of Skyrim, delegations gather with their own voices, stories, and songs. You do not need to know a single Dragon Shout to join us — just bring your music.

Our theme: An Elder Song Awakens. Not a battle for the throne, but a journey where every new song becomes part of the legend.

The Augury · A model's forecast

Who might win?

A speculative Grand Final forecast combining 2026 semi-final signals with the results of the previous editions. These are model-generated chances, not bookmaker odds or actual results.

How the forecast works

Reading the signs

Our historical-form forecast combines three factors: 60% recent semi-final strength, 30% past final results from WV189–218 (mostly the current juror's results, with a smaller realm-history component), and 10% recent champion history from WV209–218. Recent editions receive greater weight, and the results are normalized to 100% across 24 finalists.

For pre-qualified Russia and the United States, the semi-final component starts at a neutral baseline. Their jurors' history—including Gera/Gera11 and RainyWoods—is used for the other factors.

These are illustrative model probabilities, not bookmaker odds or live voting data. The confidential qualifier scores, positions and voter counts are not stored in this app.
